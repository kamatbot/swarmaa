Set up your swarm

Swarmaa gives your agents one shared memory and task queue, kept on this Mac. Pick what to connect; you can re-run this any time.

Dry run: nothing will be changed. You will only see what would happen.

Services

Other apps

Projects

Folders the swarm may read. Each becomes a scope named project:<folder>. project:demo (a scratch sandbox) always exists, and your interactive agents also get project:general, a scope with no folder.

Remote access

Grok and Dot are hosted, so they reach Swarmaa through a public HTTPS address. Setup will open Cloudflare in your browser so you can authorize a tunnel for it.

A domain you control in Cloudflare, for example api.swarmaa.com.
