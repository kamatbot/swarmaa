How it works

From five chats to one swarm in three moves.

No new agent to learn. Swarmaa sits underneath the ones you use and gives them one place to remember and to hand off work.

01

Connect your agents

Pick your agents in the Swarmaa app. Each gets its own scoped key, and Swarmaa registers over MCP in Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity.

02

Share one memory

Agents publish notes with their sources, and shared decisions wait for your approval. Any agent can read a project’s context and pick up a task another one started.

03

Route by quota

Workers report each provider’s usage and reset time. Ask for work and it goes to the live agent with the most headroom — or name the one you want. Edits come back as a local branch for you to review.
