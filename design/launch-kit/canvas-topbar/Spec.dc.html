00 — BUILD SPEC

ONE ROW. EVERYTHING ON IT.

The system title bar goes. Content runs to the top edge, and a 52 pt row carries the traffic lights, search, the open bot and its actions. Empty parts of the row drag the window.

01 — COLUMNS, LEFT TO RIGHT
Rail · 72
Ink. Its top 52 pt holds the traffic lights, centred in the row. Mark and items move down below them.
List · 300
Search field, 32 pt, fills the width; ⌘K hint. Lime + New bot, 32 pt circle, replaces the bottom New bot bar.
Thread · fill
Bot pill (menu) → status → connection state → spacer → “N needs you” → star → settings.
02 — MEASUREMENTS
Row 52 pt · paper · 2 pt ink rule below
Controls 32 pt · 1.5 pt ink outline · pill
Padding 12 pt sides · 10 pt between
Column rule 2 pt ink, runs through the row
Bot name 14 pt semibold · moji 24
Status mono 11 pt · tracked · 8 pt dot
03 — FILLS
Lime: New bot, a toggle that is on, the bot pill while its menu is open.
Flame: “needs you”, ink text on it. Never flame text.
Butter: reconnecting. Coral chip for offline.
Paper: every other icon button. White: search and the bot pill.
04 — BEHAVIOUR
  • Empty row areas drag the window; double-click zooms, following the Mac’s title-bar setting.
  • Every control in the row takes clicks. Traffic lights stay centred through resize and full screen.
  • Bot menu: Bot settings ⌘, · Rename… · Star/Unstar · Earlier messages · Hide bot.
  • Settings open: back · “Name / Settings” · settings button on.
  • Not live: the bottom strip moves into the row as a chip, “updated N min ago” and Retry.
  • Tight width: status text hides (dot stays, labelled), “N needs you” becomes a count.
  • Tasks and Usage: display title, one-line summary, their existing search on the right.
